# Business Term Definitions & Question Evaluation Set

This document establishes canonical business definitions and provides a standardized 30-question evaluation benchmark for the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce database (`olist.sqlite`).

---

## 1. Business Term Definitions & Sensible Defaults

| Business Term | Intended Meaning / Chosen Sensible Default | Rationale & SQL Implementation Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Revenue** | **Product Revenue (Item Prices)**: `SUM(order_items.price)` | Represents net merchandise volume (GMV-Items) generated by catalog products. If a business question asks for *Total Order Value / Gross Invoiced Outlay*, include freight: `SUM(price + freight_value)`. If financial reconciliation across payment methods is requested, use `SUM(order_payments.payment_value)`. Unless specified, default to **Item Prices** (`SUM(order_items.price)`). |
| **Successful Order** | **Delivered Orders Only**: `orders.order_status = 'delivered'` | Unfulfilled orders (`canceled`, `unavailable`, `invoiced`, `processing`, `created`, `approved`, `shipped`) do not represent finalized transactions and should be excluded from commercial reporting. |
| **Customer** | **Distinct Human Customers**: `COUNT(DISTINCT customers.customer_unique_id)` | In Olist, `customer_id` is a per-order surrogate key. To track true unique buyers, repeat purchasing, retention, and customer lifetime value (LTV), count distinct `customer_unique_id`. Use `customer_id` only as a join key linking `orders` to `customers`. |
| **Delivery Time** | **Purchase to Customer Receipt**: `julianday(order_delivered_customer_date) - julianday(order_purchase_timestamp)` | Measures the complete customer-facing fulfillment experience in days. (When carrier transit efficiency alone is evaluated, use carrier dispatch to customer receipt: `julianday(order_delivered_customer_date) - julianday(order_delivered_carrier_date)`). |
| **Monthly Reporting** | **Purchase Month**: `strftime('%Y-%m', orders.order_purchase_timestamp)` | Standard e-commerce practice attributes commercial volume to the date of transaction booking/placement rather than delivery completion, matching marketing spend and customer acquisition periods. (Delivery month is reserved for logistics throughput reviews). |
| **Canceled / Unavailable Orders** | **Excluded by Default** | Filtered out (`order_status NOT IN ('canceled', 'unavailable')`) for sales, GMV, volume, and performance KPIs. Included only when the explicit analytical intent is failure rate, cancellation trends, or stock-out analysis. |

## 2. Benchmark Questions (30 Starting Evaluation Cases)

### Category A: Simple (Counts, Filters, Sorting) — 8 Questions

ID: Q01
Question: How many orders have each order status?
Meaning/assumptions: Count total orders grouped by `order_status` from the `orders` table across the complete dataset.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q02
Question: How many unique customers have placed at least one order in the dataset?
Meaning/assumptions: Count distinct `customer_unique_id` values associated with records in the `orders` table joined with `customers`.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q03
Question: What are the top 5 most frequently used payment types by transaction count?
Meaning/assumptions: Aggregate records in `order_payments` grouped by `payment_type`, ordered by row count descending, limited to 5.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q04
Question: How many distinct product categories are listed in the product catalog?
Meaning/assumptions: Count distinct non-null values of `product_category_name` in the `products` table.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q05
Question: What are the 5 highest-priced individual items ever sold?
Meaning/assumptions: Retrieve the top 5 records from `order_items` ordered by `price` descending, including `order_id`, `product_id`, and `price`.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q06
Question: How many orders in the system have an order status of "canceled"?
Meaning/assumptions: Simple count filter on `orders` where `order_status = 'canceled'`.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q07
Question: What is the distribution of customer review scores from 1 to 5?
Meaning/assumptions: Count records in `order_reviews` grouped by `review_score` (values 1 through 5), ordered by score.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q08
Question: How many distinct sellers are registered in the sellers table?
Meaning/assumptions: Count distinct `seller_id` values from the `sellers` table.
Expected behavior: answer

### Category B: Intermediate (Joins, Grouping, Dates) — 12 Questions

ID: Q09
Question: Which five customer states have the most delivered orders?
Meaning/assumptions: Join `orders` and `customers` on `customer_id`. Filter for `order_status = 'delivered'`. Group by `customer_state`, order by count descending, and limit to 5.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q10
Question: What was the monthly total of item prices for delivered orders in 2017?
Meaning/assumptions: Join `orders` and `order_items` on `order_id`. Filter `order_status = 'delivered'` and `order_purchase_timestamp` between `2017-01-01` and `2017-12-31`. Group by purchase month (`YYYY-MM`) and sum `order_items.price`.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q11
Question: What is the average freight value paid per order item for each customer state?
Meaning/assumptions: Join `order_items`, `orders`, and `customers`. Filter `order_status = 'delivered'`. Group by `customer_state` and compute `AVG(order_items.freight_value)`.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q12
Question: What is the average delivery time in days for delivered orders, broken down by customer state?
Meaning/assumptions: Join `orders` and `customers` on `customer_id`. Filter `order_status = 'delivered'` where both `order_delivered_customer_date` and `order_purchase_timestamp` are not null. Compute `AVG(julianday(order_delivered_customer_date) - julianday(order_purchase_timestamp))` per `customer_state`.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q13
Question: How many delivered orders were completed later than their estimated delivery date?
Meaning/assumptions: Count records in `orders` where `order_status = 'delivered'` and `order_delivered_customer_date > order_estimated_delivery_date`.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q14
Question: Which 10 product categories generate the highest total item sales revenue for delivered orders?
Meaning/assumptions: Join `order_items`, `products`, `orders`, and optionally `product_category_name_translation`. Filter `order_status = 'delivered'`. Group by category name (preferably English), calculate `SUM(order_items.price)`, sort descending, limit 10.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q15
Question: What is the average review score for each payment type?
Meaning/assumptions: Join `order_payments` and `order_reviews` on `order_id`. Group by `order_payments.payment_type` and calculate `AVG(order_reviews.review_score)`.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q16
Question: How many orders contain items supplied by more than one distinct seller?
Meaning/assumptions: From `order_items`, group by `order_id` having `COUNT(DISTINCT seller_id) > 1`, and count the total number of such multi-seller orders.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q17
Question: What was the monthly count of new customers making their first purchase in 2017?
Meaning/assumptions: Join `customers` and `orders`. For each `customer_unique_id`, find their earliest `MIN(order_purchase_timestamp)`. Filter first-purchase dates occurring in 2017, and group by month (`YYYY-MM`).
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q18
Question: What is the average number of items per delivered order?
Meaning/assumptions: Join `orders` and `order_items`. Filter `order_status = 'delivered'`. Count items per `order_id`, then compute the overall average item count across orders.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q19
Question: Which five seller states fulfilled the highest total number of sold items for delivered orders?
Meaning/assumptions: Join `order_items`, `sellers`, and `orders`. Filter `order_status = 'delivered'`. Group by `sellers.seller_state`, count line items, order descending, limit 5.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q20
Question: What is the total payment value collected across each installment count bracket (1 installment, 2-5, 6-10, and >10)?
Meaning/assumptions: From `order_payments`, categorize `payment_installments` into brackets using a CASE statement, sum `payment_value` per bucket, and sort by installment tier.
Expected behavior: answer

### Category C: Complex (Multiple Aggregations, Window Functions, Comparisons) — 6 Questions

ID: Q21
Question: Which product categories had the highest average review score among categories with at least 100 reviewed orders?
Meaning/assumptions: Join `orders`, `order_items`, `products`, `order_reviews`, and `product_category_name_translation`. Filter `order_status = 'delivered'`. Group by product category name (English), filter `COUNT(DISTINCT orders.order_id) >= 100`, compute `AVG(review_score)`, and sort descending.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q22
Question: What was the month-over-month percentage growth in delivered order revenue throughout 2017?
Meaning/assumptions: Aggregate total item sales (`SUM(price)`) for delivered orders per month in 2017. Use a window function (`LAG`) to compute the percentage revenue change from the previous month: `((revenue - prev_revenue) / prev_revenue) * 100`.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q23
Question: Who are the top 3 sellers by total item revenue within each of the top 5 product categories?
Meaning/assumptions: Identify the top 5 categories by total delivered item price. For those 5 categories, rank sellers using `DENSE_RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY category ORDER BY revenue DESC)` and return sellers ranked 1 through 3.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q24
Question: What percentage of repeat customers (customers with more than 1 lifetime order) leave a review score of 4 or 5 compared to one-time customers?
Meaning/assumptions: Group orders by `customer_unique_id` to partition customers into two cohorts: repeat (`COUNT(order_id) > 1`) and one-time (`COUNT(order_id) = 1`). Calculate the percentage of reviews with `review_score >= 4` for each customer cohort.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q25
Question: For customer states with at least 500 delivered orders, what is the late delivery rate and the average delivery delay in days for late orders?
Meaning/assumptions: Join `orders` and `customers`. Filter `order_status = 'delivered'` and states with `COUNT(*) >= 500`. Calculate the percentage of orders where `order_delivered_customer_date > order_estimated_delivery_date`, and compute `AVG(julianday(order_delivered_customer_date) - julianday(order_estimated_delivery_date))` strictly for those late orders.
Expected behavior: answer

ID: Q26
Question: Across product categories with at least 200 items sold, what is the average product weight in kilograms and the corresponding average freight-to-price ratio?
Meaning/assumptions: Join `order_items`, `products`, `orders`, and category translations. Filter `order_status = 'delivered'` and category item count `>= 200`. Calculate `AVG(products.product_weight_g) / 1000.0` and `AVG(order_items.freight_value / order_items.price)`.
Expected behavior: answer

### Category D: Clarification Cases (Ambiguous Requests Requiring Dialogue) — 4 Questions

ID: Q27
Question: Which sellers performed best?
Meaning/assumptions: The term "performed best" is fundamentally subjective and multi-faceted. It could indicate highest total revenue (GMV), highest order volume, highest customer review ratings, lowest fulfillment delay rate, or lowest order cancellation rate. The system cannot execute a deterministic SQL query without user clarification on the metric and time window.
Expected behavior: ask for clarification

ID: Q28
Question: What is our customer churn rate this quarter?
Meaning/assumptions: In non-subscription e-commerce, "churn" lacks an inherent definition. It requires specifying an inactivity threshold (e.g., 90, 180, or 365 days without purchase) and a defined baseline comparison quarter. Furthermore, the dataset covers historical Brazilian e-commerce transactions (2016–2018), so "this quarter" is ambiguous without a calendar anchor.
Expected behavior: ask for clarification

ID: Q29
Question: Show me all the bad orders from last month.
Meaning/assumptions: "Bad orders" is undefined—it could mean canceled orders, orders with negative customer reviews (rating <= 2), orders delivered after the estimated delivery date, or orders with refund/payment disputes. In addition, "last month" is ambiguous without a reference anchor date relative to the dataset's historical timeframe.
Expected behavior: ask for clarification

ID: Q30
Question: How profitable was our electronics category recently?
Meaning/assumptions: The database contains transaction item prices and shipping freight fees, but does not track Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), seller commissions, marketplace take-rates, or operating expenses necessary to calculate actual "profit". Additionally, "recently" is unanchored, and "electronics" spans several distinct category names (`eletronicos`, `informatica_acessorios`, `telefonia`, etc.).
Expected behavior: ask for clarification
